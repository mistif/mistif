# Copy patterns

Fix by being specific, not by swapping one buzzword for another.

| Pattern | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| inflated verb | "Unlock the power of your data" | "See which customers stopped ordering last month" |
| generic call to action | "Get started today" | "Import your first spreadsheet" |
| hidden actor | "Mistakes were made during the migration" | "We ran the migration before testing it" |
| an abstraction that thinks | "The platform understands your needs" | "It reads your past orders and suggests a reorder" |
| forced triple | "Fast, simple, powerful" | one true claim with a number |
| "not X but Y" | "It's not a tool, it's a partnership" | say what the partnership consists of |
| chat opener | "Great question! Let me explain" | start with the answer |
| empty ending | "Hope this helps!" | stop |

## Voice
If the person gave a sample of their writing, match its length, vocabulary and punctuation. Keep their odd specific details. Do not sand everything smooth.

## Checks
- Every claim traces to a source the person gave you.
- Read it aloud: where you would not say it, change it.
- Rescan for em and en dash characters before handing over.
